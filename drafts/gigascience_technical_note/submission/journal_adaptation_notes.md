# Future journal adaptations

This directory is fixed to the GigaScience Technical Note structure. A future
target receives a sibling draft directory and copies verified material rather
than changing these sources in place.

## Nature Methods Article

The current Findings can be split into an unheaded introduction, Results and
Discussion. The abstract must be reduced to at most 150 words. The current
three-figure narrative fits the Article display-item allowance, while detailed
benchmark preparation can become Online Methods and Extended Data.

A Nature Methods submission would need a stronger case for broad methodological
importance and general applicability beyond variant annotation throughput. It
should retain the fast-annotator result and correctness evidence centrally.

## Nature Methods Brief Communication

This would require a distinct, unsectioned main text of approximately 1,200
words and at most two principal display items. It should not be produced by
mechanically truncating the Technical Note; the story and figure composition
would need to be rewritten around one central claim.
